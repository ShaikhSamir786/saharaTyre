Retreading Technology

Precision Cold vs Hot Process Retreading

ac_unit

Cold Process (Precure Retreading)

Factory-moulded, pre-vulcanized high-density tread rubber is bonded to the buffed tyre casing with cushion gum under vacuum pressure inside an autoclave chamber at 105°C.

  • check_circle Preserves original steel cord integrity
  • check_circle Ideal for long-distance highway transport
  • check_circle Available in 10.00-20 and 295/80 R22.5 sizes
local_fire_department

Hot Mould Retreading

Uncured rubber is applied from shoulder to shoulder and vulcanized inside a full tyre mould at 150°C, producing a completely seamless new tread pattern with reinforced sidewalls.

  • check_circle Deep aggressive traction lugs for off-road terrain
  • check_circle High cut & chip resistance for chemical plant dumpers
  • check_circle Available in 11.00-20, 12.00-24, and tractor sizes
Fleet Safety Protocol

Recommended Axle Placement for Remould Tyres

do_not_disturb_on

Steer / Front Axle

Never use remould tyres on steer axles. Always mount brand-new radial tyres on front steering positions for maximum control.

check_circle

Drive Axle (Dual Mount)

Highly recommended. Heavy traction block retreads provide maximum pulling power on drive axles at half the price.

check_circle

Trailer / Dummy Axle

Optimal economy. Rib retread patterns provide free-rolling low resistance and exceptional fuel economy on multi-axle trailers.
