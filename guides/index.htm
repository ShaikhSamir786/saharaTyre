Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions our mechanics and tyre technicians receive.

Which tyre condition is best for personal cars in Vapi? expand_more

For expressway driving (NH48, Mumbai-Ahmedabad route) and family cars, brand new tyres from MRF, CEAT, or Apollo offer maximum wet braking and full manufacturer warranty. For city-only commuting and budget constraints, 5-point safety tested pre-owned tyres with 4mm+ tread offer up to 60% savings.

Are remould (retread) tyres safe and legal for commercial trucks in India? expand_more

Yes, commercial cold precured retreading is 100% legal in India and used globally by major transport fleets. Retreaded tyres are recommended for drive and trailer axles, while brand new tyres are recommended for the front steer axle.

What minimum tread depth should I look for in a second-hand tyre? expand_more

Never purchase a tyre below 2.5mm tread depth. At Sahara Tyre Vapi, our pre-owned tyres are tested to verify a minimum of 3.5mm to 6mm tread depth with zero bead damage, inner liner cracking, or sidewall bulges.

Can I mix new and used tyres on the same vehicle? expand_more

Yes, provided that tyres on the same axle (both front or both rear) have identical tread depth and construction (radial vs bias). We always recommend mounting the newest, deepest-tread tyres on the rear axle to prevent oversteer in wet conditions.
