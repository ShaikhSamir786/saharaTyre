When You Should Buy Brand New

  • check You frequently drive on highways (NH48) or travel long inter-city distances.
  • check You are replacing front steering tyres on cars or commercial trucks.
  • check You want official 3–5 year manufacturer warranty protection.
  • check You plan to keep the vehicle for more than 2–3 years.

When Second-Hand Is the Smart Choice

  • check You need an affordable replacement for local city commuting in Vapi/GIDC.
  • check You need an emergency spare stepney tyre for unexpected punctures.
  • check You are selling or returning a leased vehicle within 6–12 months.
  • check You operate multi-axle truck trailers and need cost-effective trailer tyres.
