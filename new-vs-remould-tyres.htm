Fleet Best Practices

The 3-Tier Commercial Tyre Lifecycle Strategy

How top fleet operators in Gujarat maximize tyre investment through systematic rotation.

1

Stage 1: Virgin Steer Tyres

Buy brand-new premium radial tyres (MRF, Apollo, CEAT) for front steer positions. Run until tread reaches 4mm, preserving the underlying carcass casing.

2

Stage 2: 1st Cold Retread (Drive Axle)

Apply cold precured high-traction lug tread to the tested casing. Move the tyre to the rear drive axle for heavy torque transfer and long haulage.

3

Stage 3: 2nd Retread (Trailer Axle)

Apply ribbon/highway tread pattern for the second retread and position on multi-axle trailer positions. Squeeze maximum kilometer return from a single casing.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions: New vs Remould

Are remould (retreaded) tyres safe for heavy commercial trucks?

Yes, certified precure (cold) retreaded tyres are completely safe and widely used by global airlines and commercial transport fleets on drive and trailer axles. However, industry safety standards strictly mandate that front steer axles should always run brand-new tyres.

How much can a transport fleet save by using remould tyres?

Commercial transport fleets typically save 50% to 65% in tyre procurement expenses by retreading quality radial casings (such as MRF, Apollo, or CEAT) 2 to 3 times, dramatically reducing overall cost-per-kilometer (CPKM).

What is the mileage difference between a new tyre and a cold retread tyre?

A premium cold remould tyre delivers approximately 75% to 90% of the mileage of a brand-new tyre when applied on a structurally sound, laser-tested casing and operated under correct air pressure.
