Our Puncture Repair Process

1

Leak Detection

Complete surface inspection and bubble water test to locate nails, screws, thorns, or bead leaks.

2

Precision Repair

Application of high-tensile rubber string plugs or vulcanized mushroom patches that bond permanently.

3

Pressure & Seal Test

Re-inflation to manufacturer PSI specifications, valve check, and final pressure retention confirmation.

Can you fix a sidewall puncture? expand_more

Sidewall punctures cannot be safely repaired with a simple plug due to the immense flex and heat on the tyre sidewall. For your highway safety, our technicians will inspect the tyre and recommend the most economical, safe replacement option (new, quality used, or remoulded).
