Our 5-Point Quality Inspection for Second Hand Tyres

Buying used tyres doesn't mean compromising on road safety. At Sahara Tyre in Vapi GIDC, we maintain a strict testing protocol so you can drive with absolute peace of mind:

speed 1. Digital Tread Depth Check

We verify that every tyre has substantial legal tread depth (usually 4mm to 7mm remaining) across the entire contact patch.

compress 2. High-Pressure Leak Test

Each tyre is inflated beyond operating pressure to verify carcass integrity, bead sealing, and identify micro-punctures.

visibility 3. Sidewall & Bead Inspection

We inspect for sidewall bulges, cuts, rim chafing, or cord damage. Any tyre with sidewall weakness is immediately rejected.

balance 4. Wheel Spin & Runout Test

We mount and spin test the tyre to ensure zero radial or lateral runout, guaranteeing smooth vibration-free rolling.
