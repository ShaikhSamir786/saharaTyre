Our Rigorous 5-Point Safety Inspection

We reject any tyre showing structural fatigue, uneven shoulder wear, or hidden bead tears.

1

Tread Depth

Digital gauge verification for minimum 3mm to 6mm usable rubber.

2

Pressure Test

High-pressure submersion tank test to detect microscopic leaks.

3

Inner Liner

Full internal tactile audit for cord separation or patch history.

4

Bead Integrity

100% inspection of steel bead wires for rim-sealing safety.

5

Sidewall Check

Zero bulges, cuts, curb gouges, or age-cracking permitted.
