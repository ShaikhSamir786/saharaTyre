Used vs Remould Tyres: Which Budget Option is Best?

Looking for maximum value without overpaying for brand-new rubber? Compare 5-point tested second-hand tyres against precision cold retread (remould) tyres on price, remaining tread life, and vehicle application.

Quick Decision Summary: Used vs Remould Tyres Choose inspected used tyres for passenger cars, stepney spares, and low-mileage city driving where OEM compound flexibility is critical. Choose remould (retreaded) tyres for heavy commercial trucks, trailers, and tippers where full 12mm–16mm tread depth delivers the lowest cost-per-kilometer.

Frequently Asked Questions: Used vs Remould

Which lasts longer: a used tyre or a remoulded tyre?

A remoulded tyre lasts significantly longer than a typical used tyre because it comes with full factory tread depth (10mm to 16mm) bonded onto a tested casing, delivering 60,000 to 90,000 km in commercial truck applications compared to 15,000 to 30,000 km remaining on a second-hand tyre.

Can I use remould tyres on passenger cars?

For passenger cars, inspected second-hand tyres with 4mm+ genuine tread are generally preferred over remoulds due to higher speed ratings, lower road noise, and better high-speed stability. Remould tyres are primarily engineered for commercial trucks, trailers, tippers, and LCVs.

What is the price difference between used and remould tyres in Vapi?

Both used and remould tyres cost 40% to 65% less than brand-new tyres. While used tyres are priced based on remaining tread millimeters, remould tyres offer superior cost-per-kilometer value for commercial transport operators.
